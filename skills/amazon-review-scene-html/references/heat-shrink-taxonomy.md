# Heat-shrink tubing scene taxonomy

Use this reference only for heat-shrink tubing review analysis. Retain the hierarchy:

`Main Scene → Sub Scene → Specific Application`

## Main scenes

1. **Automotive / Vehicle**: passenger car, pickup truck, SUV, RV, trailer, motorcycle, ATV/UTV, vehicle battery, automotive lighting, vehicle wiring, vehicle repair.
2. **Marine**: fishing boat, bass boat, pontoon, sailboat, personal watercraft, boat battery, marine lighting, marine electronics, boat wiring, bilge pump, navigation system.
3. **Home Appliance**: kitchen appliance, small appliance, power cord, appliance wiring, household equipment, appliance repair.
4. **Power Equipment**: lawn mower, generator, pressure washer, snow blower, chainsaw, small-engine equipment, garden equipment.
5. **Outdoor Lighting**: Christmas lights, landscape lighting, garden lighting, patio lighting, decorative lighting. Lighting belonging to a vehicle or boat stays in that device scene.
6. **Industrial Equipment**: industrial machinery, factory equipment, motor, industrial control equipment, machine wiring, manufacturing equipment.
7. **Electrical Installation**: electrical panel, control panel, control cabinet, terminal connection, building wiring, electrical wiring, wire splicing, professional electrician work. Automotive splicing remains Automotive / Vehicle.
8. **Electronics**: charging cable, speaker, audio equipment, electronic device, low-voltage electronics, electronic repair. A generic wire/cable/connector is insufficient.
9. **Other**: an explicit actual use that does not fit the above.
10. **Not Mentioned**: insufficient actual-use evidence.

## Specific applications

Normalize explicitly mentioned applications to concise labels, including Battery Cable, Battery Terminal, Engine Bay Wiring, Sensor Wiring, Lighting Wiring, Trailer Lighting, Deck Light Wiring, Navigation Light, Bilge Pump Wiring, Power Cord Repair, Wire Splicing, Terminal Connection, Cable Repair, Connector Protection, and Solder Joint Protection. Otherwise use Not Mentioned.

## Evidence examples

- “Great waterproof heat shrink.” → Not Mentioned.
- “Used it to waterproof the wiring on my fishing boat.” → Marine → Fishing Boat → Boat Wiring.
- “Used it on my F-150.” → Automotive / Vehicle → Pickup Truck → Not Mentioned.
- “Used this on my trailer lights.” → Automotive / Vehicle → Trailer → Trailer Lighting.
- “Used it to repair a lawn mower wire.” → Power Equipment → Lawn Mower → Wiring Repair.
- “I used it on my truck battery and my boat wiring.” → two rows: Automotive / Vehicle → Pickup Truck or Vehicle Battery → Battery Wiring; Marine → Boat → Boat Wiring.

Normalize F-150/Ford F150/Ford pickup/truck/pickup truck → Pickup Truck; trailer lights/trailer lighting → Trailer Lighting; boat battery/marine battery → Boat Battery; lawn mower/riding mower → Lawn Mower; charging cord/charging cable → Charging Cable.
