# Amazon US Product Video Creative

Use the upstream strategy brief as the source of truth. This skill develops creative expression; it does not silently replace product/VOC research or promote an unverified claim to a selling point.

## Start with a creative evidence card

Extract only:

- confirmed product facts and selected SKU/variant;
- target audience, task, scene, and purchase obstacle;
- core point that can be shown, rather than merely said;
- approved proof assets and required proof shots;
- prohibited/untested claims; and
- visual, brand, logistical, or safety constraints.

If the strategy brief is missing, request it or make a clearly bounded mini-brief from user inputs. Do not fabricate a differentiated benefit just to make an idea feel complete.

Read [creative proof patterns](references/creative-proof-patterns.md) when deciding which creative territory fits the evidence.

## Develop concepts

Create at least five genuinely distinct directions when the evidence permits. Good distinctions are pain resolution, real-task workflow, operation demonstration, result inspection, structural explanation, and context-specific use. Different titles with the same visual narrative do not count as different concepts.

For every direction, state:

- concept name, target user, pain, single core message;
- opening hook, main visual action, and proof method;
- tone/rhythm, ending, duration fit, cost/difficulty; and
- any proof shot that must be captured before the claim can be used.

Favor actual operation, before/after conditions, task completion, and detail close-ups. A rotating product or parameter recital is only acceptable as supporting footage.

## Duration planning

Plan three separate treatments, not one stretched edit:

- **15 seconds:** one result, one task context, one visually clear proof.
- **30 seconds:** one primary message plus up to two supporting proof points; show an intelligible action sequence.
- **60 seconds:** a full task or decision journey, core operation, key objection, and result/boundary check.

For each duration, propose at least three hooks: a visible action, a user task/pain, and a visible result. Each hook needs the first 1–3 seconds, natural English on-screen copy, VO need, and the bridge into the main treatment.

## Deliverable

Lead with a comparison matrix evaluating pain fit, proof strength, brand fit, US-user clarity, production feasibility, and reuse value. Then deliver:

1. a recommended main concept and a backup;
2. separate 15s/30s/60s creative outlines with narrative, message order, visual style, caption system, sound direction, ending, difficulties, and required materials;
3. an Agent-3-ready creative execution brief with approved facts, required visual proofs, prohibited claims, and production limits.

Do not create a shot-by-shot, second-by-second script here; hand that to `amazon-video-storyboard`.

Default to Markdown. Render HTML only when the user asks for a shareable/browser artifact.

## Boundaries

- Do not use low-price urgency, false scarcity, or unverified “best” claims as the default creative idea when the brand seeks professional value.
- A scene must be a realistic use context, not a decorative stock backdrop.
- If accessories or performance conditions differ by child SKU, keep the creative SKU-specific until confirmed.
- Do not describe competitor footage as viewed unless it was actually reviewed.
