# Amazon US Product Video Storyboard & Production

Translate a selected creative concept into a shootable plan. The finished deliverable must allow a production crew to capture all necessary footage with ordinary ecommerce-production equipment.

## Preflight before writing frames

Confirm or explicitly flag:

- marketplace, actual child SKU, real package contents, and product facts;
- operating method, materials/consumables, correct settings, and scene boundaries;
- required proof shots and claims that cannot be made;
- whether the task involves electrical, sharp, heated, heavy, moving, or other hazardous operation.

If an item is unknown, mark it `CONFIRM BEFORE SHOOT` in every affected shot. Do not fill an operations gap with a plausible-looking action. Read [production safety and timing](references/production-safety-and-timing.md) before planning tools, electrical items, or performance comparisons.

## Storyboard requirements

Build **independent** 15s, 30s, and 60s tables. Every table must include:

1. shot ID;
2. continuous start/end time and shot duration;
3. shot size and camera angle;
4. visual/action description;
5. camera movement;
6. natural US-English on-screen text and voiceover;
7. sound/music direction;
8. product point and observable fact being proved;
9. props/preparation; and
10. transition/edit instruction.

Calculate and visibly verify the total duration of each version. Give complex operation enough real time; do not hide the key step in a time-lapse, fast motion, or jump-cut. A shorter cut should have a narrower message, not merely delete random steps from the long cut.

For tool videos, prefer a meaningful subset of: product overview, moving structure, hand operation, contact/work area, real consumable, setup/adjustment, result inspection, actual confirmed accessories, and a realistic non-hazardous work context.

## Production package

After the storyboards, include:

- prop/equipment/safety checklist;
- an efficient shoot order grouped by set, lighting, and camera position;
- mandatory master shots reusable across all edits;
- likely failure points and reshoot coverage;
- postproduction requirements for captions, sound, grade, brand, formats, and exports;
- complete English VO for each duration with Chinese review translation when the output language is Chinese; and
- a durable file naming and folder convention.

Finish with an execution self-check covering factual consistency, physical shootability, single-message clarity, timing, proof value, continuity, and platform formats.

Default to Markdown. Render HTML only when the user asks for a browser-ready handoff document.

## Boundaries

- Never stage a dangerous or incorrect operation. For electrical/electronic tools, use de-energized demonstrations unless the verified manual and a qualified operator establish a safe alternative.
- Do not turn a visible cosmetic result into proof of electrical performance, force, safety, certification, durability, waterproofing, or comparison superiority.
- Accessories, included tools, and compatibility claims must belong to the actual filming SKU.
- Label any failed/comparison sample honestly; never imply it represents a competitor or customer product without evidence.
- This skill executes an approved direction. For strategy use `amazon-video-strategy`; for concept creation use `amazon-video-creative`.
