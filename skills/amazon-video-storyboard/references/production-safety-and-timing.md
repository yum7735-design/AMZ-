# Production Safety and Timing

## Capture safety

- Follow the actual manual and a qualified operator’s method for setup and operation.
- Use de-energized, unpowered, and secured demonstration materials for electrical tasks unless the approved brief explicitly establishes a safe, controlled alternative.
- Do not show hands in pinch, cutting, heating, rotating, or striking zones without proper operating technique and protection.
- Stop rather than improvising if the required consumable, setting, accessory, or safety control cannot be confirmed.

## Evidence capture

- Record one uninterrupted master take for any operation used as proof, from material positioning through the observable result.
- Capture the same key action from a primary and a safety angle when hands may block the evidence.
- For visual comparisons, match materials, preparation, lighting, camera distance, and stated conditions. Clearly label demonstration samples.
- Capture 5–8 second clean plates for graphics/caption versions and isolated natural sound for key mechanisms.

## Timing checks

- Add every shot duration before delivery. Do not leave a gap or overlap.
- Protect enough time for the viewer to see the setup, action, and result; small mechanisms and inspection details usually need at least 1–2 seconds of stable screen time.
- Preserve the natural operation at normal speed when it is the proof. Use slow motion only as an additional detail view, never as the only operation evidence.
